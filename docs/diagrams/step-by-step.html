Hidden ≠ free

  • Thinking and every tool request are output tokens, billed at the highest rate, even though the shopper never sees them.
  • In S32, about half the cost (₹0.66 of ₹1.34) is output, mostly Claude's reasoning and its tool requests.

Why S38 costs 6× more: the snowball

  • Each round re-sends the whole conversation: question, Claude's earlier requests and every tool result so far.
  • So input grows round after round (≈60 → 1,000 → 5,600 → 8,300 → 8,700 → 9,200 new tokens). Rounds 3–6 alone account for nearly all of S38's input cost.
  • The fixed prompt is cheap thanks to caching (₹0.45 for 6 reads).

What's agentic, in one line

S32: two decisions (which search, which 5), no reaction to results. A fixed pipeline could do it.

S38: picks the tool order, follows the "if not" branch, runs tools in parallel, judges results, retries 3 ways, recovers from 0, and decides when to stop. That's what the extra ₹6.50 bought.
